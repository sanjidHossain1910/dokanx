# Operations feature checks

Use a spare test shop or keep a backup of the current shop before manual testing.

- Customer: make a due sale, open Customers → ledger, collect a partial amount, confirm the remaining balance and original receipt. Retrying the same transaction must not collect twice.
- Supplier: make a partial/due purchase, open Purchase → Supplier ledger, record a payment and check invoice remaining due. Pending receipt status must not change when paying a supplier.
- Return: open Sales history → receipt → Return products. Try fractional units, a partly paid invoice, a second return, and a damaged item with restocking disabled. Confirm refund/due reduction, inventory and net daily sales/profit.
- Dashboard reports: Sales must open history rather than New Sale. Search by product name and open a historical receipt. Profit must open a sale-by-sale history with each invoice's original total price and total profit. Switch week/month/custom dates, search invoice/customer/product, then tap a sale to see all its product photos and recorded price/quantity/cost/profit/margin entries. Multiple sales of the same product must remain separate; multiple products in one invoice must stay in one history row. Confirm recorded retail/wholesale prices survive catalog edits. The sale history shows original sale profit before later returns and shop expenses, with returns listed separately; it must match the original sale detail. The dashboard still accounts for return-day adjustments and expenses. Product profit excludes tax, and margins with zero/negative revenue show a dash. Profit history and sale profit details always show amounts and margins with no Show/Hide control. Dashboard summary cards retain their existing 20-second reveal behavior.
- Pricing: add/edit a product with Wholesale disabled and confirm no wholesale choice appears in its inline sale card/cart. Enable it with a positive price, test retail/wholesale switching for fractional and counted quantities, then verify totals, stock, cost and old receipts. Disable wholesale while a wholesale cart exists; checkout must require switching it to Retail.
- Inline sale cards: Add must reveal quantity controls in the card with no bottom sheet. Test all seven unit groups, direct typing and Done/blur, decimal input, plus/minus, zero removal, stock limits, storage failure/reset, and changing Retail/Wholesale. Unsaved/invalid input must not navigate to checkout; a stale edit must not recreate a cleared or replaced cart. Scanning must reveal the matching card. English units must stay short throughout forms, cards, reports and receipts (kg, g, L, m, hr), without changing stored unit IDs or Bengali labels.
- Favorites: tap the standalone corner star in New Sale, filter Favorites, reload, then unpin. The star must use a transparent background and no border; favorite is gold-filled and non-favorite is muted outline. The image remains full-width.
- Held sales: build a cart with a customer, discount and tax; hold it and start another sale. Confirm no stock/payment/sale changed on hold. Open the clock icon, search held carts, and resume through review. A currently open cart must be kept intact. Try price changes, disabled wholesale, expired/insufficient stock and a failed storage write. Held photos and labels must survive backup/restore.
- Cash: open Reports/Profile → Daily cash. Enter opening cash, record cash and wallet transactions, count cash and close the day. A difference requires a note. Further cash transactions that day must be refused; wallet transactions remain available.
- Expiry: open Products → Expiry alerts or a product's Batch & expiry button. Assign existing untracked stock, then test yesterday's expiry, today's expiry, FEFO selection, returns and stock reductions. Add a batch to a pending purchase and receive it twice; stock/lot must only increase once.
- Backup: Profile → Backup & restore → Create → Save/share. Restore on a separate test install from the setup welcome screen. Verify shop activation, IDs, product/shop photos, QR images, drafts and all histories. Corrupt/truncate a file and verify rejection. Change shop data while a restore review is open and verify it cannot replace newer data.
- Printer: pair an ESC/POS Bluetooth printer in Android settings, install a development/release build with the native module, choose it under Profile → Receipt printer, choose 58/80 mm and test. Print an older sale and verify its original date/ID and final `Thank you`. Expo Go must show an unavailable message for Bluetooth; system print and PDF sharing remain available. Bluetooth receipt output is currently Android-only; iPhone uses system print/PDF. The bundled driver does not provide reliable BLE discovery/write readiness for an iPhone Bluetooth flow.

Automated tests cover financial calculations, retries, corrupt history, batch allocation, backup archive validation, native file staging/cleanup and raster output. Android JS export does not replace native compilation or physical printer/camera testing.


- Stock Count: Products → Stock Count → New count. Leave some rows blank, enter 0 for an empty shelf, use piece steppers and decimal kg/L inputs, then review. Reasons are required for differences. Save once and verify original/counted quantities, note, date and photos in history and product stock movements. Repeat with matching quantities, changed stock during review, expired batches, a failed storage write and a retried save. Unsaved forms require confirmation on exit. Verify count history and photos after backup/restore, and that sales/profit/payment totals do not change. Added stock remains unassigned to batches; reductions consume earliest-expiry batches first.

- Security: Profile → Security & Privacy. Both locks start off. Test App Lock with the phone screen lock and every supported biometric. Background/foreground and changing screens must retain the unlocked session; force-close/restart must require verification again. Configure a separate report PIN, confirm matching/mismatched inputs, and verify that reports, sales history, old receipts, return history, customer ledger, daily cash and backup screens stay protected on direct navigation. Dashboard amount reveals must also require report authorization. A freshly completed sale must still show/print its own receipt. Verify five wrong attempts, persisted cooldown after restart, cancellation and storage errors. Forgot PIN must show the OS prompt before setting a NEW PIN; normal PIN unlock must not offer a phone-auth bypass. Security changes/disable require fresh phone verification even in an unlocked session. Verify secure settings and PIN hashes are absent from shop backups and are not replaced by restore. Fingerprint/face/phone-passcode authentication requires real-device QA; iOS Face ID requires a development/release build rather than Expo Go.

Four-digit PIN regression: create a four-digit PIN including a leading zero; the fourth tap advances automatically, then matching confirmation auto-saves. Cold restart and enter the same four digits: auto-unlock must occur with no submit button. Wrong PIN clears the dots and retains the lock/cooldown. A legacy six-digit record must offer phone-verified four-digit setup; cancelling or failed OS authentication must leave its verifier and report protection intact.

Smart restock checks: Home / Products / Purchase > Smart restock; report PIN gate also protects deep links. With >=7 in-stock complete days and >=3 sales days, verify quantities, lead/safety days, current stock, returns and pending orders. Today must affect stock without diluting the daily average. New products and inconsistent unit/history data must allow manual quantities. Edit global history/target/lead/safety/budget and product pack/lead/shelf-life/max-order/supplier rules; existing manual list entries must stay unchanged across recalculation, restart and .dokanx backup/restore. Check pieces and fractional kg/L. In Buy list, change/remove quantities, see missing-price/stale-product warnings, select supplier and item subset, then verify exactly one purchase draft is created and only selected entries leave the list. Existing drafts must remain intact. Check light/dark, Bangla/English, small-screen scrolling/keyboard and save-error retry. The Online AI source sheet must explicitly say unconnected and never show fabricated predictions.

## Optional collection follow-ups

Tablet layout checks: test phone widths 360/390, tablet portrait 768/820 and landscape 1024/1366, plus a narrow split-view window. Profile's photo/shop card, statistics and all menu rows must move in the same ScrollView, with Help reachable above the floating tab bar. Tablet menus use two columns; on narrow windows they stack. Sales/purchase grids use 2/3 columns on phones and 4/5 on wider windows with no clipped quantity controls. Inventory uses two columns only when cards have sufficient width. Rotate while editing a cart quantity and confirm keyboard dismissal/remount does not leave other product cards disabled. Check dark/light and Bangla/English. Form fields and bottom sheets stay centered at readable widths; the tab bar stays centered and reachable. Full iPad/orientation behavior requires a native rebuild; Expo Go and bundle checks cannot verify release-device configuration.

- New Sale → Due: optional date switch starts off. Enabling it opens a native date picker; phone reminders are a separate opt-in. Cash/fully paid sales never receive a plan.
- Reminder time is authored at 9 AM in the phone local timezone; selecting today after 9 schedules about 30 seconds later. OS power saving can delay delivery. Date-only plans never request notification permission.
- Home → Payment follow-ups: search customer/phone/invoice and filter Today, Overdue, Upcoming. The list and collection ledger retain Sales & Profit access protection. Notification text contains no names, balances or payment details; tapping opens the protected list.
- Customer ledger: edit each unpaid invoice date/reminder; turn off the date switch and save to remove. Collect action opens the existing collection sheet. Allocations continue to use the existing oldest-invoice-first rules.
- Partial collections show remaining invoice due; full collection or returns clearing the debt remove the follow-up from active views and cancel/dismiss its phone notification. Remaining invoices can be given a new date.
- Dates persist in SQLite and local backups. Restore cancels unrelated old reminders and reconciles only future unpaid plans. Past dates remain overdue in-app and are not resent in a burst. Delivery receipts stay on this phone and prevent duplicate notifications after reload.
- If permission is denied/revoked, dates still save and a visible notice links to phone settings. Scheduling/storage errors show Retry without undoing a completed sale. Scheduling is bounded to the nearest 60 outstanding requests; a visible notice asks the user to open the app regularly to queue later dates. No notifications are sent to customers.
- Native test on Android/iPhone: grant/deny permission; set today, wait for reminder; reload before/after delivery; pay before deadline; edit/remove date; restore another backup; check foreground/background and notification tap through App Lock/report PIN. New native builds must include expo-notifications and the native date picker. Expo Go supports local notifications; remote push registration is not used. Emulator checks do not replace device delivery tests.

SDK 57 Expo Go compatibility finding: the current test client exposes ExpoNotificationChannelManager but lacks NotificationsChannelsProvider. setNotificationChannelAsync rejects with a native null-cast error. The local loader now checks both before evaluating notification wrappers. Date-only follow-ups remain available and do not show a global scheduling failure; opting into unsupported phone reminders shows a compatibility notice. Native support must be validated in a compatible development/release build; a Metro restart or granting permissions cannot add this missing provider. [due-reminders] development logs distinguish module loading, channel creation, permission reads/requests and synchronization failures without logging customer/financial payloads.
